Voice Recorder Plus is a focused audio recorder forked from Fossify Voice Recorder.

Features:

- Record, pause, resume, save, discard, and play recordings.
- Save recordings in the Voice Recorder Plus folder.
- Optional automatic recording after launch.
- Optional Record and email mode using a configured backend sender.
- Custom filename pattern, audio source, bitrate, sample rate, themes, widgets, and recycle bin.

Record and email requires a real backend endpoint. Android email apps cannot silently send messages or report confirmed delivery, so this app does not open an email composer for that flow.
